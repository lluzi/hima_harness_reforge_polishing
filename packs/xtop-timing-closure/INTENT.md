## Business

This Pack takes a read-only Innovus checkpoint and uses XTop, Innovus, StarRC and PrimeTime to push
setup and hold violations until they are clean or measured progress converges. It outputs the best
admitted Innovus checkpoint and retains the complete experiment history.

## Golden Flow

The calibrated Golden Flow is the verified SWERV28 Foundation Flow described in
`knowledge/source-flow.md` and hash-bound by `knowledge/source-manifest.txt`. It proved two full
PT-to-XTop-to-Innovus-to-StarRC-to-PT rounds. It is method evidence, not a customer result template.

## Answers

The business input is an Innovus `.enc.dat` checkpoint with its restore script. The Site supplies
the process, library, extraction, PT scenario and tool identities. Each generation studies endpoint
deltas before selecting a bounded fix plan. The business output is the best refreshed Innovus
checkpoint, not the most recent attempt. Successes and failures both become Campaign experience.

## Ambiguities resolved

XTop post-opt values are predictions. Only the refreshed PrimeTime result after Innovus `loadECO`,
physical commands, ecoRoute and StarRC is admitted. “No longer progresses” is measured convergence,
not a claim of sign-off closure. Existing PG DRC, connectivity and unconstrained endpoints remain
visible and cannot be erased by timing improvement.

## Knowledge applied

The Pack applies the source flow's marker/log/artifact rule, same-physical-version handoff,
per-generation ECO prefix, independent iteration directories and like-for-like PT comparison. It
adds endpoint fixed/remaining/entrant/regressed feedback and AI plan revision inside the existing
Workshop and Fabric surfaces.
