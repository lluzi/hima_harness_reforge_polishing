# Agentic Timing Closure System 0.4

## Business

One durable business flow delegates the whole XTop fix-timing engineering Task to a task-scoped
resident engineering agent. The resident receives the fixed design/R1 identity, actual reports
and full method. It researches, writes scripts, operates authorized
XTop, measures and keeps the best actual state, and returns reusable engineering material.

The ambition is to repair every target setup/hold violation under the fixed problem and deliver
the best measured result, residuals, limitations and reusable artifacts. Global collateral and regression checks
remain separate; their UNKNOWN or known failure prevents an unqualified adoption/signoff claim. A complete best-effort delivery may leave residuals;
the engineering Task can complete while the downstream Goal stays false.

## Golden Flow

One sequence runs prepare-inputs → prepare-baseline → fix-timing → evaluate-timing → deliver.
Preparation uses actual binding/readiness and common-R1 helpers; evaluation consumes the single
committed Reader result; delivery returns explicit goalMet, reports and reusable engineering package.
Runtime supplies durable execution and artifact identity. The Pack supplies finite business protocols.


The method baseline is retained native XTop timing data for the same staged design, followed by
common initial AutoFix as a repair step, and one resident whole-engineering repair from common R1. The golden flow supplies evidence and reproducible inputs; it is not copied
as hard-coded design objects or a mandatory physical tail.

## Answers

Repair effect is setup/hold WNS, TNS, violations, regressions and supported collateral checks under
the fixed input identity. Independent serial-AutoFix comparisons belong to the external evaluator, not the product task or
Goal. Cost, duration, calls and seats are not engineering victory conditions. Complete best effort is
allowed; missing evidence stays unknown. The task-scoped resident may use normal coding, analysis and
authorized XTop methods, and must return scripts/ECO/checkpoint/reproduction evidence.

## Ambiguities resolved

The route is XTop-only. Native WNS/TNS, violation counts, transition/capacitance/fanout/legality
checks, ECOs and checkpoints are engineering evidence. They do not become tc_final physical-signoff
facts. No new PrimeTime, Innovus or StarRC producer is a prerequisite or mandatory tail.

Version 0.2.10's six branches, retained lead and physical referee remain in legacy/0.2.10
contract/graph/semantics snapshots and the existing helpers/tests. Version 0.4 does not reinterpret
their evidence.

## Knowledge applied

resident-timing-playbook.md defines the complete autonomous method and delivery shape. Since 0.4.1 it
adds the full-closure ladder for multi-scenario GBA designs (base choice, measurement of record,
electrical first, fail-reason-driven next actions, structural classification, clock skew rules,
protected structures, proof), distilled from a 2026-10-07 study on the XTop vendor tutorial. 0.4.2 adds the
rule that a picosecond residual is not a floor until the listed residual tactics were measured. 0.4.3 adds
time-box discipline, a step-by-step pipeline-skew procedure and a small-delivery rule. 0.4.4 ships
the ladder itself as xtop-closure-ladder.tcl.txt, a design-independent procedure library the resident
runs first, with obligatory legalization and an overlap gate. 0.4.5 fixes the ladder's transition-target
collection and states that inputIdentity carries exactly its four identity keys. 0.4.6 states that every
remaining/regressed/blocked/unknown item is an object and that a Reader repair rewrites only the result
document, because published support files are immutable. 0.4.7 makes common R1 the vendor's own
automatic-fix flow from the XTop tutorial (transition and capacitance first, then setup and hold, twice),
so the before state is the strongest stock-XTop reference rather than setup and hold fixers alone.
0.4.8 (demo branch `demo/atcs-derive`) withdraws the executable ladder and the playbook's step-by-step
ladder and keeps only general principles and XTop command mechanics, so the resident must work out the
closure method itself on the design. 0.4.9 adds the general clock-skew technique for structural setup
walls and a time-box check before delivery, after a 0.4.8 resident closed hold but stopped at setup 55
with a clock attempt that used data buffers without obligatory legalization.
xtop-capabilities.md supplies native tool mechanics without prescribing a design answer.
state-and-evidence.md keeps actual, predicted, unknown and final signoff meanings separate.
