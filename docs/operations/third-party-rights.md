# Third-party software rights

The bundled components keep their applicable licenses. The actual distribution includes its npm/native
inventory, original notices, license texts and fixed corresponding source, patches and build instructions
under `third-party/`. These materials are available with the local distribution without an extra charge.
The corresponding-source manifest identifies each retained file by SHA256. Its reconstruction notes
explain the upstream recipes and any technical qualifications still outstanding for this candidate.

Recipients may exercise all rights granted by these licenses. HimaHarness terms do not restrict
modification or replacement of LGPL-covered libraries, or reverse engineering for debugging those
modifications. Retain original copyright notices and the applicable license/disclaimer texts when
redistributing covered material. Modified libraries must preserve the application interface to load.

The local technical candidate uses ad-hoc macOS signing. Replacement instructions include the signing
steps needed for a modified local bundle; do not present that candidate as notarized or commercially
signed. Any future transaction triggering license installation-information requirements must include
that information. Current technical test results cannot qualify a later signing or delivery policy.

The original LGPL/GPL texts and component licenses govern these rights. The dependency inventory and
materials are evidence of the shipped components and their sources, rather than a warranty of legal
clearance or of untested platforms.
