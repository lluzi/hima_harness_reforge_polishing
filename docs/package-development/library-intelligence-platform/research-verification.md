## Reader-value review

A fresh senior engineer should be able to answer, from the retained artifacts:

1. DigWise is primarily a multi-PVT metric/Cell-decision workbench; Solido is primarily a mature
   characterization/QA/outlier/compare suite.
2. Parser, dashboards, rule counts, ML outliers, revision compare and Python APIs are table stakes.
3. Liberty explicitly describes conditional abstract/electrical models, but design adoption and chip outcomes require
   other source domains.
4. Hima's proposed differentiation is the replayable chain from source facts to current design exposure, independent
   falsification, safe candidate action and matched commercial response.
5. The proposal stops if API qualification fails, identity cannot be reproduced, or three preregistered reviews do not
   preserve high-impact recall while improving review time or invalid top investigations by at least 30% on one metric.

The most decision-relevant remaining gaps require private/current product evidence, live licenses, customer trials,
real revision reviews or API qualification. Another broad marketing search is unlikely to change the first-slice
decision.
