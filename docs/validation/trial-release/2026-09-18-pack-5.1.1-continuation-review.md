# Pack 5.1.1 continuation Campaign review

Date: 2026-09-18

Verdict: `PARTIAL`. Pack 5.1.1 fixed the split Liberty/DB blocker and passed real LC, DC,
custom synthesis and adoption. It did not reach matched post-route E0 because two later Pack defects
and one Site rediscovery defect stopped the tested Runs.

## Proven progress

- `SPLIT_LIBRARY_FIX_PASS`: `FOUNDRY_LIB` was `.lib`, `FOUNDRY_DB` was a distinct `.db`;
  Library Compiler and Design Compiler passed with no `DB-1`.
- A single-output generation reached adoption with 10 custom masters and 259 instances.
- The parallel free-mining fork and Workshop retry path remained functional.

## Root causes

1. `hima_site rediscover` fed a saved Permit with nine read roots through an eight-root discovery
   transport limit and failed before contacting the host.
2. Multi-output generation correctly produced one `_MO` Cell with `Y0/Y1` output pins, while free
   portfolio validation and cumulative-shard bookkeeping independently reconstructed two physical
   Cells named `_Y0` and `_Y1`.
3. The Site declared maximum routing layer `M7`, but Innovus 23.14
   `setNanoRouteMode -routeTopRoutingLayer` accepts an integer route level. The unwrapped Tcl error
   returned Innovus to its prompt, so the Job idled toward a two-hour timeout.

## Corrective scope

- Increase only the existing bounded discovery-root transport to 64 entries; Permit authority and
  review/save behavior are unchanged.
- Make `expected_generation_jobs` the single physical Cell identity projection for portfolio,
  mapping census and cumulative shards.
- Convert reviewed `M<number>` Site names to the integer Innovus argument at script rendering.
- Execute init and P&R Tcl through a generated `catch` wrapper that exits nonzero on script errors.

No graph, research strategy, Budget, Site Permit authority or new component is introduced.
